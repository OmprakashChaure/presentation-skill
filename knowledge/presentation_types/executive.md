# Executive Presentation Type

## Purpose

Define type-specific policies for executive/leadership presentations.

## Scope

Board meetings, steering committees, leadership reviews, C-suite updates, decision briefings.

## Core Policies

### Executive Answer First

- **MUST** lead with the answer/recommendation (Slide 1 or 2)
- **Format:** "Recommendation: [Action] — Expected impact: [Metric] — Investment: [Resource]"
- **Rule:** No "background first" — executives read summary, skip to decision

### Implications Over Details

- **MUST** focus on: Business impact, Strategic alignment, Risk, Resource requirement
- **SHOULD** relegate technical/operational detail to appendix
- **Rule:** If it doesn't change the decision, it doesn't belong in main deck

### Decision Frame

- **MUST** specify: Decision needed, Options considered, Recommendation, Rationale
- **Format:** "Decision: Approve/Reject/Defer [Proposal] by [Date]"
- **Options:** Status quo + 2–3 alternatives (with trade-offs)

### Recommendation with Trade-offs

- **MUST** present: Recommended option + Why not others
- **Trade-off table:** Criteria × Options (Cost, Time, Risk, Strategic fit, Reversibility)
- **Rule:** No recommendation without acknowledged downsides

### Business Impact Quantification

- **MUST** use audience's metrics: Revenue, Margin, Cash flow, Market share, NPS, Risk exposure
- **Format:** "Expected ΔRevenue: +$XM (p50: +$YM, p90: +$ZM)"
- **Rule:** No "strategic value" without proxy metric

### Risks & Mitigation

- **MUST** include: Top 3–5 risks, Likelihood, Impact, Mitigation, Owner
- **Format:** Risk register table (Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Owner | Residual)
- **Rule:** No "no risks" — at minimum: execution risk, adoption risk, dependency risk

### Next Steps — Concrete

- **MUST** specify: Action, Owner, Deadline, Success criterion
- **Format:** "[Owner] to [Action] by [Date] → Success: [Measurable outcome]"
- **Rule:** No "follow up," "discuss," "align" as next steps

## Slide Type Defaults

| Purpose | Slide Archetype |
|---------|-----------------|
| Executive summary | Pyramid (Answer → 3 Arguments) |
| Decision frame | Recommendation slide |
| Options + trade-offs | Comparison table |
| Business impact | KPI/Metric + Chart |
| Risk register | Table |
| Roadmap | Timeline (phased) |
| Appendix | Reference (technical detail) |

## Narrative Default

- **Primary:** Executive Decision pattern
- **Alternative:** Pyramid (if pure synthesis/recommendation)

## Design Adaptations

- **Density:** Low — high whitespace, large type, minimal text
- **Builds:** Minimal — static slides preferred (printed/pre-read)
- **Colour:** High contrast, status colours (RAG) for health indicators
- **Charts:** Simple — bar, waterfall, KPI tiles; no complex multi-series
- **One message per slide:** Strictly enforced

## Anti-Patterns

- Background/context before answer
- Technical detail in main deck
- >15 slides for 30-min slot
- No clear decision ask
- Risks minimized or omitted
- Vague next steps ("socialize," "align")
- Unquantified "strategic" claims
- Dense text slides

## Handoff to Other Layers

- **Reasoning:** Selects executive decision pattern; enforces answer-first
- **Narrative-patterns:** Executive decision + Pyramid for summary
- **Slide-types:** Recommendation, Comparison, KPI, Risk register archetypes
- **Visualization:** Simple charts, status indicators, waterfall
- **Evaluation:** Checks: Answer first, decision framed, trade-offs shown, risks quantified, next steps concrete
