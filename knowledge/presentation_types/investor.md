# Investor Presentation Type

## Purpose

Define type-specific policies for investor/pitch presentations.

## Scope

Seed, Series A+, growth equity, IPO roadshow, strategic investor updates.

## Core Policies

### Standard Sequence (MUST follow order)

```
1. PROBLEM — Pain point, quantified
2. CUSTOMER — Who, how many, evidence of demand
3. SOLUTION — Product, differentiation, demo/screenshot
4. MARKET — TAM/SAM/SOM, bottom-up, source cited
5. TRACTION — Actual metrics (not projections), cohorts, retention
6. BUSINESS MODEL — Unit economics, pricing, CAC/LTV, payback
7. ECONOMICS — P&L drivers, margin trajectory, path to profitability
8. DIFFERENTIATION — Moat, IP, network effects, switching costs
9. RISKS — Top risks, mitigation, honesty builds credibility
10. PROJECTIONS — 3–5 year, assumptions explicit, scenario range
11. ASK — Raise amount, terms, use of funds, milestones funded
```

### Strict Metric Classification (MUST label every number)

| Label | Definition | Examples |
|-------|------------|----------|
| **Actual** | Measured, auditable, historical | Revenue LTM, ARR, Users MAU, Churn % |
| **Projection** | Modelled future, assumption-driven | Year 2 Revenue, Year 3 Margin |
| **Assumption** | Input to projection | Market growth %, Conversion %, Price increase |

**Rule:** NEVER present projection as actual. NEVER invent traction.

### Traction Evidence Standards

- **Revenue:** Net revenue retention, cohort curves, concentration (top 10 %)
- **Product:** Usage depth (DAU/MAU), feature adoption, NPS
- **Sales:** Pipeline velocity, win rate, sales cycle, CAC payback
- **Team:** Key hires, advisors, board composition
- **Rule:** Screenshots ≠ metrics. "Interest" ≠ LOI ≠ Revenue.

### Market Sizing — Bottom-Up Required

- **MUST** show: TAM → SAM → SOM with calculation logic
- **Format:** "[# Customers] × [Price] × [Frequency] = $XM"
- **Sources:** Industry reports (cited), primary research, analogies
- **Anti-pattern:** "Market is $100B" with no path to capture

### Differentiation — Moat Focus

- **MUST** answer: Why you? Why now? Why defensible?
- **Types:** Tech IP, Network effects, Data moat, Switching costs, Brand, Distribution
- **Rule:** "Better UX" is not a moat without evidence

### Projections — Assumption Transparency

- **MUST** show: Base case + Downside + Upside
- **MUST** list: Top 5 assumptions driving variance
- **Format:** "Assumption: Enterprise attach rate 15% → Sensitivity: ±5% = ±$20M ARR"
- **Rule:** Hockey stick without assumption breakdown = low credibility

### The Ask

- **MUST** specify: Amount, Instrument (SAFE/Equity/Debt), Valuation/Cap, Use of funds (% per category), Milestones this round unlocks
- **Rule:** No "strategic investor" without strategic value defined

## Slide Type Defaults

| Purpose | Slide Archetype |
|---------|-----------------|
| Problem | Assertion-Evidence (quantified pain) |
| Solution | Image-led (demo/screenshot) + Assertion |
| Market | Chart (TAM/SAM/SOM waterfall) |
| Traction | KPI tiles + Cohort chart |
| Unit economics | Table (CAC, LTV, Payback, Margin) |
| Financials | Chart (Revenue/Expense/Cash) |
| Team | Image-led (photos + 1-line credibility) |
| Ask | Recommendation slide |

## Narrative Default

- **Primary:** Investor pattern (Problem → Customer → Solution → Market → Traction → Model → Economics → Differentiation → Risks → Projections → Ask)

## Design Adaptations

- **Density:** Medium — investors read decks alone (pre-read critical)
- **Charts:** Clean, labelled, source-cited; no chartjunk
- **Appendix:** Extensive — detailed financials, cap table, technical IP, market backup
- **Brand:** Professional, not flashy; credibility > creativity

## Anti-Patterns (MUST PREVENT)

- Invented/fabricated traction or market data
- Projections presented as facts
- Top-down market only ("1% of $100B")
- No cohort/retention data for recurring revenue
- CAC/LTV not shown or not credible
- Team slides without relevant credibility signals
- Ask without use-of-funds breakdown
- "Conservative projections" that aren't

## Handoff to Other Layers

- **Reasoning:** Enforces sequence, metric classification, assumption transparency
- **Slide-types:** Maps investor sequence to archetypes
- **Visualization:** Cohort charts, waterfall, unit economics tables
- **Data-storytelling:** Strict actual vs projection separation
- **Evaluation:** Checks: Sequence, metric labels, traction evidence, market bottom-up, assumption disclosure, ask completeness
